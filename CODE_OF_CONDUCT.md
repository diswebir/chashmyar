# Community expectations

We want discussions and contributions to be respectful and constructive. Please:

- Treat people fairly and assume good faith; criticism should address the work, not the person.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized content or disclosure of private information.
- Keep reports relevant and redact personal data and credentials.
- Respect maintainers' decisions and other contributors' boundaries.

Maintainers may moderate, edit or remove content and restrict participation to protect the community. For vulnerabilities, use the private reporting route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). For other serious conduct concerns, ask a maintainer for a private channel without posting sensitive details publicly.
